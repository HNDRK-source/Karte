## How it works

A *Verticillium* spore formulation, harmless to the elm, is introduced into the trunk and triggers a defence reaction that protects the tree against *Ophiostoma novo-ulmi* for one season.

## Application

- Timing: spring, at bud break
- Method: injection into the young annual ring, minimally invasive
- Repetition: **annual**, preventive, for **healthy** trees
