## 1. Consultation

Get in touch with a DutchTrig® partner in your region.
→ see [About → Partners](/en/ueber-uns/)

## 2. Self-monitoring

Pay attention to:

- **Disease pressure** in the area (diseased elms nearby, freshly felled elms, bark-beetle flight)
- **Health of your elm** (wilting branches, discoloration, deadwood)

When in doubt, call in specialists.

## 3. Keeping the tree healthy – as required

- Tree care and site improvement
- Commission an external arboricultural company (e.g. RAL-certified)

## 4. Vaccination – as required

Where disease pressure in the surroundings is elevated: annual DutchTrig® vaccination.

→ see [DutchTrig®](/en/dutchtrig/)
