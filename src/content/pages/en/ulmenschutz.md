Elm protection only works as a combination of measures. No single action is enough — what matters is the integrated programme.

## 1. Monitoring

- Identify and register elms worth protecting
- Assess the level of disease pressure in the surroundings
- Continuously monitor tree condition

## 2. Keeping trees healthy

- Professional tree care
- Site improvement (soil, water, sealing)

## 3. Vaccination

Biological prevention with DutchTrig® — annual, prophylactic.
