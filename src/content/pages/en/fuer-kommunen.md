## 1. Consultation

Initial contact with a DutchTrig® partner in your region.
→ see [About → Partners](/en/ueber-uns/)

## 2. Tree inspection & monitoring

- Filter for **elms** in the municipal tree register
- Assess **disease pressure** in the surroundings
- Regular monitoring by own staff **or** external inspectors

## 3. Keeping trees healthy

- Tree care and site improvement
- By own arborists **or** an external arboricultural company (e.g. RAL-certified)

## 4. Vaccination – as required

Where disease pressure is high or for particularly valuable specimens: annual DutchTrig® vaccination.

→ see [DutchTrig®](/en/dutchtrig/)
